This methods project covers blood perfusion and brain extraction for histological analysis. It describes anaesthesia, thoracic-cavity access, PBS perfusion to remove blood, and paraformaldehyde fixation to preserve tissue structure.

The report then outlines careful craniotomy and brain extraction, including freeing the optic nerves and spinal-cord attachment before preservation.
