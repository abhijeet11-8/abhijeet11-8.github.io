This project documents GMR-GAL4 × UAS TER94 fly crosses, recording phenotypes including eye and wing defects across overexpression, RNAi, and mutant lines.

It also analyses Drosophila Activity Monitor data, comparing basal activity and daily activity patterns between wild-type and mutant flies, including early-riser and late-riser behaviour.
