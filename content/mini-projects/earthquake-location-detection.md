This project reviews earthquake location as a nonlinear inverse problem: estimating the hypocentre and origin time by comparing observed and predicted seismic-wave arrival times across stations.

It compares L2 and L1 residual norms, likelihood-based approaches, and grid search. The report also discusses computational cost, non-smooth optimisation, and the challenge of complex Earth velocity models.
