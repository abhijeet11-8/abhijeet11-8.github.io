This experiment amplified a TERT-gene region containing the rs2853677 SNP, which is associated with cellular senescence, telomere maintenance, and oncogenesis.

The workflow covered primer design, annealing-temperature optimisation, PCR, agarose-gel electrophoresis, QIAquick gel extraction, and NanoDrop quantification. The combined extracted DNA concentration was 26.75 ng/µL.
