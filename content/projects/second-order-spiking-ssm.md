This NeurIPS 2025 TS4H Spotlight work applies SHaRe-SSM to wearable devices. It achieved state-of-the-art results on human-activity-recognition and PPG-DaLiA EEG datasets recorded from phones and smart watches while being 52.1× more energy efficient.

Kartikay Agrawal, Abhijeet Vikram, Vedant Sharma, Vaishnavi N, Ayon Borthakur
