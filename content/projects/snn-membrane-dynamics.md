This project examines spiking neural-network membrane dynamics and optimisation objectives. It studies leaky integrate-and-fire neurons, surrogate-gradient training, and the effect of initial membrane potential on firing patterns and performance.

The work proposes learnable initial membrane potentials, a last-time-step loss for static tasks, and label-smoothed TET loss for neuromorphic tasks. The experiments found that the modifications changed firing behaviour while keeping inference overhead low.
