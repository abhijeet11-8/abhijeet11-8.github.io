Semester project with Prof. Chaitanya Guttikar at IISER Pune (Jan–Apr 2025). I worked with the FER2013 dataset for facial emotion recognition using a ResNet-like architecture and achieved 71.25% accuracy.

This was the first model I trained on real-world data. The dataset was quite noisy, with poorly separated classes, significant class imbalance, and a lot of bias. The *Happy* and *Sad* classes had a particularly large number of samples compared to some of the other classes. I haven’t retrained or revisited the model since then, so it’s nice to look back on it now as a reminder of where I started.
