This report covers undergraduate neurobiology experiments with Dr. Nixon Abraham: optogenetics, mouse handling and training, blood perfusion, brain extraction, calcium imaging, and Backyard Brains electrophysiology.

The data analysis found improved odor discrimination during training at 14.62 microwatts, with task discrimination times around 0.92–0.96 seconds. Cockroach-leg recordings also showed a clear increase in action-potential amplitude after stimulating leg bristles.
