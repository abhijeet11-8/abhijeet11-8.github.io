This project examines how climate change can affect dispersal through temperature, wind, rainfall, cloud cover, habitat fragmentation, and species interactions.

Using butterfly movement as a case study, it explains Cox proportional-hazards models for analysing the probability that a flight bout ends. The analysis links warmer temperatures and radiation to longer flights and greater dispersal propensity, while cloudiness reduces flight activity.
