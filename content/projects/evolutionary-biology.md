Summer project with Prof. Sutirth Dey at IISER Pune (Jun–Jul 2025). I standardised the spatial kernel with the Drosophila Activity Monitor, analysed activity data, and built a larvae activity tracker to study how selection pressures on adult dispersal affect larval dispersal ability.

Key takeaways: data cleaning and analysis with SciPy, NumPy, Matplotlib, and OpenCV.
