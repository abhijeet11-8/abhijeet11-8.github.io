I am a BS-MS Interdisciplinary Sciences student at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research, Pune, expected to graduate in 2027.

My research interests include neuromorphic computing, spiking neural networks, state-space models, low-power AI systems, physics-informed neural networks, non-linear dynamics, and random matrix theory.

My work spans spiking state-space models, diffusion models, facial-expression recognition, neuromorphic computing, and evolutionary biology. I program primarily in Python (SciPy, NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, PyTorch, and JAX), as well as C++, Git, and LaTeX.

You can reach me at [abhijeet.vikram@students.iiserpune.ac.in](mailto:abhijeet.vikram@students.iiserpune.ac.in).

## Research experience

- **Master’s thesis (expected 2027):** Low-precision quantisation and pruning of state-space models for edge-computing brain-computer interfaces, supervised by Prof. Chetan Singh Thakur at IISc Bangalore.
- **Spiking state-space models (2025–2026):** Research on SHaRe-SSM, a second-order fully spiking SSM for long-sequence learning and energy-efficient wearables, with Prof. Ayon Borthakur at IIT Guwahati.
- **Diffusion models (2025):** Studied deterministic chaotic dynamics as an alternative to stochastic noising for efficient edge-oriented diffusion models, with Prof. M. S. Santhanam at IISER Pune.
- **Evolutionary biology (2023–2025):** Drosophila activity and dispersal analysis, spatial-kernel standardisation, and activity tracking with Prof. Sutirth Dey at IISER Pune.
