I am a BS-MS Interdisciplinary Sciences student at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research, Pune, expected to graduate in 2027.

My research interests include neuromorphic computing, spiking neural networks, state-space models, low-power AI systems, physics-informed neural networks, non-linear dynamics, and random matrix theory.

My work spans spiking state-space models, diffusion models, facial-expression recognition, neuromorphic computing, and evolutionary biology. I program primarily in Python (SciPy, NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, PyTorch, and JAX), and do undestand C++, Git, and LaTeX.

You can reach me at [abhijeet.vikram@students.iiserpune.ac.in](mailto:abhijeet.vikram@students.iiserpune.ac.in).

## Research experience

- **Master’s thesis (expected 2027):** Low-precision quantisation and pruning of state-space models for edge-computing brain-computer interfaces, supervised by <strong>Prof. Chetan Singh Thakur</strong> at **IISc Bangalore**.
- **Diffusion models (2026):** Studied deterministic chaotic dynamics as an alternative to stochastic noising for efficient edge-oriented diffusion models, with **Prof. M. S. Santhanam** at **IISER Pune**.
- **Spiking state-space models (2025–2026):** Research on SHaRe-SSM, a second-order fully spiking SSM for long-sequence learning and energy-efficient wearables, with **Prof. Ayon Borthakur** at **IIT Guwahati**.
- **Evolutionary biology (2024–2025):** Drosophila activity and dispersal analysis, spatial-kernel standardisation, and activity tracking with **Prof. Sutirth Dey** at **IISER Pune**.
